# Landing surface brief

- **Scope:** `/` marketing page, full redesign. Mode: Persuade.
- **Audience:** security leads and analysts evaluating the demo. **Action:** open the console or start the demo.
- **Proof/content:** the version gate (a 9.8 on absent software is suppressed; a matched Log4j 2.14.1 scores 99.4), the six-input score breakdown, the traceable pipeline, the seeded asset ledger. All demo data labelled.
- **Constraints:** static React/Vite, existing router and demo sign-in, 390–1600 px, reduced motion, no secrets.
- **Chosen direction:** black-site operations room (user-selected). Cinematic 3D server monolith under live attack; soft ambient teal light, amber alarm, red attack packets. Data-as-typography, hairline rules, grain, no glass-card stacks.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Aegis is a night-shift operations room where an intrusion is staged literally: almost every exploit is filtered at the version gate, and the rare matched one breaks through and lights the exact server. The category ships abstract globes, particles and card grids; this page refuses abstraction and makes the correlation mechanism physical.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black ink (#040607) field; bone text (#e9e7de); phosphor teal (#53e3cb) for shield and telemetry; signal amber (#f0a94a) for matched danger; red-orange (#ff5b4d) only on live attack packets. Oversized Archivo display with tight tracking, Instrument Serif italic accents, JetBrains Mono data. Hairline rules, grain, corner telemetry, soft ambient light; no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no icon-card grids.

**STORY:** The visitor understands that CVSS is noise and version-aware correlation is the signal; believes only this product shows why a 9.8 gets suppressed while a 10.0 matters; opens the console.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed night server room, a 3D rack under attack on the right bleeding off-canvas, shield impacts and a scripted matched breach. Headline bottom-left over a dark grade, two CTAs, a hairline telemetry ledger along the bottom. A live incident terminal sits low-right; no eyebrow above the headline. The signature interaction is the attack scene plus its incident log, with a manual "trigger attack" control.

**FORM:** Code-led, direction pinned by the user (black-site ops room); no concept roll. Signature scene built as a real-time Canvas perspective scene, not a static image.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
